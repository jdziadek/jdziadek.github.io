Dead Fuse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how personal data is used in Dead Fuse on Android, iOS and PC and on this website. Available features depend on the version, store and region: some test builds do not include advertising or purchases.

Who is responsible and how to contact us

The controller of personal data processed for Dead Fuse is Jakub Dziadek, an individual based in Poland. For privacy questions and requests, email loboogamesstudio@gmail.com. Service providers may also process some information for their own purposes, as explained below.

Local saves and anonymous service profiles

You do not need to register with an email address or password. Your nickname, settings, progress, daily match allowance, rewarded-ad credits and full-version entitlement are saved locally. Local game progress is not synced between devices or between Android and iOS.

Online services use Unity Authentication to create or sign in to an anonymous service profile with a unique player identifier. “Anonymous” means that you do not supply login details; it does not mean that service data cannot be linked to that identifier. A separate installation or device may have a different profile.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data can remove local saves and access to the anonymous profile. It does not automatically delete server-side data or leaderboard entries. Request deletion before uninstalling where possible. A purchase may be restored through the original store and store account; a purchase is not a cross-platform save or entitlement.

Rankings and online play

Eligible Battle Royale and Arena results can be submitted automatically to the global rankings, including after a single-player match. Results recorded offline may be queued on the device and sent when connectivity returns. This functional reporting is separate from optional Unity Analytics and does not depend on analytics consent.

Ranking reports include the service player identifier, nickname, match identifier, game version, mode and relevant results such as score, placement, kills, Arena waves, completion status and time played. Reports also include rules and validity indicators, such as whether a development or training mode was used. Unity Cloud Code checks reports and updates Unity Leaderboards; service logs may include player identifiers and report errors. These records support rankings, duplicate-report prevention and result integrity.

Rankings are visible to other players. Your nickname, position and results may be displayed publicly and are associated with a service identifier. Avoid putting your real name, contact details or other private information in your nickname or lobby name.

For multiplayer, Unity services, including Authentication, Multiplayer Sessions/Lobby and Relay, process connection and session information. This can include IP addresses, service identifiers, nickname, lobby name, game version and platform, player count, game settings, connection status and gameplay messages. Other players can see lobby information, nicknames and gameplay needed to participate. Network and diagnostic information is used to connect players and operate and protect these services.

Player accounts, friends and co-op

In versions that support accounts, you can link your Unity player ID to Google Play Games on Android or a Unity account on PC. This lets you recover the online profile on another device. You can also use an account transfer code. Saved local progress and store purchases follow the rules above.

Your nickname and friend code (nickname with a number) can be visible to other players in rankings, friends lists, profiles and online matches. Nicknames must follow the Player Rules. We store friends, requests and blocks. Friends can see whether you are in the menu or in a match while both have the game open.

Player profiles can show scores, rankings and up to 10 recent ranked matches. Online matches and co-op Arenas use Unity Relay, so the game does not send your IP address to other players. Quick chat sends the numbers of predefined phrases. We do not store a chat history.

Nickname reports contain the two players’ identifiers, the reported nickname and the reason. We keep reports for moderation for up to 90 days. To remove your profile and associated data, use Menu → Account → Delete account and data in versions that offer it, or visit Delete your Dead Fuse account and data.

Rewarded advertising and privacy choices

In builds with advertising enabled, Battle Royale against bots and Arenas share a daily free-match allowance. After that allowance is used, you can choose a rewarded ad for one additional match. An entry is credited when the advertising SDK confirms the reward. The daily allowance and unused credits are stored on the device. The full-version purchase removes advertising and the related match-entry limits.

We use Unity LevelPlay mediation and the advertising networks ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google AdMob, depending on platform and availability. Ad services may receive IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP, device and advertising identifiers where permitted, app and operating-system information, diagnostics, consent signals and ad views, clicks and reward interactions. They use these data for ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted by your choices and applicable law, personalised advertising.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents applicable advertising privacy messages. Advertising is requested only when the consent flow permits it. You can reopen available advertising choices under Settings → Privacy. Rejecting personalisation does not necessarily remove all ads: contextual or limited ads may still be available. Agreeing to watch a rewarded ad is separate from giving consent to personalised advertising or optional analytics.

The current iOS integration does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. We do not obtain permission to use IDFA or carry out tracking that requires ATT consent. The absence of an ATT prompt does not mean that no data are processed: the service, contextual advertising, purchase and diagnostic data described in this policy may still be used.

Where US state privacy rules apply, advertising disclosures may be considered a “sale”, “sharing” or use for “targeted advertising” under those rules. Use Settings → Privacy → Do not sell or share my personal information when that option is available to change applicable advertising choices. UMP manages the regional choices and the game passes supported opt-out signals to LevelPlay. You can also contact us about these rights or if you cannot access the controls. We do not exchange player information for direct monetary payment.

Optional gameplay analytics

We enable Unity Analytics only after you opt in. Events describe sessions, match starts and endings, mode, duration, entry-gate and offer views, ad outcomes and completed full-version purchases. In versions with the new online features, events can also describe co-op rooms, invites, match starts, revives, wave retries, predefined quick-chat phrase numbers and acceptance of the Player Rules. The SDK may also process installation or user identifiers, device and app information, approximate location and diagnostics. These data help us understand game use, reliability and the purchase and advertising flows.

Our custom gameplay events do not include your nickname, card details or purchase receipts. Activity before consent is not queued for later analytics submission. You can withdraw consent under Settings → Privacy without losing access to gameplay. Withdrawal stops further optional analytics collection and does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

The analytics deletion control in the same menu requests deletion of Unity Analytics data associated with the current installation. It does not delete Authentication profiles, rankings, multiplayer records, advertising-provider data or purchase records. Contact us for those requests, for a previous installation, or if an in-game request cannot be completed.

Full-version purchases

Where purchases are available, Google Play on Android or the Apple App Store on iOS handles payment. Unity IAP connects the game to the store. The game receives product, transaction and verification information to grant and restore the full-version entitlement. We do not receive your payment-card number or store password.

Unity IAP can process transaction or receipt information, identifiers, device and app details, approximate country and diagnostics to operate purchases and purchase reporting. This processing is separate from optional gameplay analytics. Unity states that IAP service logs are kept for 30 days; transaction information is retained as needed for the service, duplicate-purchase prevention and revenue reporting. See Unity IAP privacy and deletion information. Stores have separate payment-record retention rules.

For purchase-service data processed on our behalf, contact us so that we can handle or forward a verified request. Requests about data a store or Unity processes as an independent controller may also need to be made directly to that provider. Deletion does not itself cancel, refund or transfer a purchase.

Purposes and legal bases

Where the GDPR or equivalent rules require a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Providing the requested service / performing a contract: operating requested online sessions, verifying purchases, granting and restoring entitlements and responding to service requests.
  • Legitimate interests: operating and maintaining game rankings, checking result integrity, preventing fraud and abuse, protecting services and handling support or legal claims. These interests are balanced against players’ rights; you may object where applicable.
  • Consent: optional gameplay analytics and advertising, identifiers or device storage/access for which consent is required. Consent choices can be changed as described above.
  • Legal obligations: retaining or disclosing information when required by applicable law, including responding to valid legal requests and privacy-rights requests.

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond, solve the issue and verify relevant requests. Please do not send passwords, card numbers or unnecessary sensitive information. Essential online and purchase processing is separate from the optional analytics setting; without the necessary information those particular features may not work.

Recipients and international processing

Information is shared with providers to the extent needed for the features above: Unity and its affiliates (Authentication and Unity Player Accounts, Player Names, Friends, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards, multiplayer connectivity and Relay, Analytics, IAP, LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads); Google and its affiliates (AdMob, UMP, Google Play and Google Play Games when you link your Google Play Games account); Apple and its affiliates (App Store purchases); and GitHub for this website. Other players receive the public ranking, profile, friend and multiplayer information described above. We may disclose relevant information to authorities where legally required and to advisers or providers assisting with a verified request or legal claim.

Providers act as processors/service providers or independent controllers depending on the activity. Their policies explain their own processing and recipients. Processing can take place outside your country, including outside the EEA or UK. Where required, transfers must use an applicable safeguard, such as an adequacy decision or contractual safeguards. You can contact us about transfers of data for which we are responsible.

How long information is kept

  • Local saves and pending reports: until replaced, submitted or deleted with the app’s local data. Clearing local data does not remove copies already sent to services.
  • Authentication and rankings: the current game does not apply a fixed automatic expiry to the anonymous profile or ranking entry. They can remain until deleted or the relevant service records are reset. A reset does not necessarily remove provider archives. Contact us to request deletion rather than relying on uninstalling.
  • Multiplayer: session information serves the active lobby or connection; diagnostic, security and provider service records can outlast a session under the provider’s retention rules.
  • Analytics: Unity states that raw events are retained for 13 months; metrics and user profiles may remain until deletion is requested. See Unity Analytics retention information.
  • Purchases and advertising: the periods depend on the provider, purpose and legal requirements, including purchase verification, accounting, security and fraud prevention. The IAP log period above does not apply to all transaction or store records.
  • Support and rights requests: for as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal or record-keeping requirements. Information needed for a legal obligation or dispute may be retained after a deletion request, with its use restricted as required.

Your rights and deletion requests

Depending on your location and applicable conditions, you may request access to information about you, a copy or portability, correction, deletion or restriction, and object to certain processing. You may withdraw consent at any time. You may also have rights to opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising, use an authorised agent and appeal a refused request. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

Send requests to the contact address above. We may need limited information to locate the relevant profile and verify that it belongs to you. A nickname alone may not uniquely identify it. Tell us which platform and installation the request concerns; do not send store passwords or payment credentials. We will explain any additional verification needed and respond within the period required by applicable law. If we cannot fulfil a request, we will explain why and any available appeal route.

See Delete your Dead Fuse account and data for the in-game and email options, which data they cover, and what may be retained. We coordinate requests with relevant processors. Independent providers may require separate requests. You may complain to your local data-protection authority. In Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Visits to this website

This information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising or analytics tools to it. GitHub receives technical request information and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under its privacy statement linked above. Website hosting data is separate from the game’s SDK data.

Children and policy changes

Dead Fuse is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to applicable law, contact us so that we can investigate and arrange appropriate deletion or other measures. Local rules on children’s data and the age for valid consent can differ.

We update this policy when features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. Where required, material changes will also be communicated in the game and new consent obtained before the affected processing starts.
