Dead Fuse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Data controller and contact

The controller of personal data related to Dead Fuse is Jakub Dziadek Loboo. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact jakubdziadek91@gmail.com.

The game and data stored on your device

You do not need a player account to play. Your nickname, settings, local progress, free-match counter, rewarded-ad entries and full-version entitlement are stored on your device. We do not sync progress between devices or between Android and iOS.

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes the local save. You can restore a full-version purchase through the store by connecting to the internet and using the store account that made the purchase.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an extra Battle Royale entry. We use Unity LevelPlay and the Unity Ads and Google AdMob networks.

Advertising services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, device and app information, diagnostics and ad interactions. This supports ad delivery and measurement, security and fraud prevention. The scope of processing also depends on your consent choices and privacy settings.

Google User Messaging Platform handles the required privacy choices. You can reopen the available advertising privacy options in the game under Settings → Privacy. Purchasing the full version disables ads.

Optional analytics

We enable Unity Analytics only after you consent. We record sessions, match starts and endings, game mode, play time, entry-gate and offer views, the outcome of ad attempts and completed full-version purchases. The SDK may include an installation identifier, device and app information, approximate location and diagnostics.

This information helps us understand whether players return and how the offer works. These events do not include your nickname, payment-card details or purchase receipt. Activity before consent is not held for later submission.

You can withdraw consent under Settings → Privacy. Withdrawal stops further analytics collection and does not restrict access to the game. In the same menu, you can request deletion of analytics data associated with the current installation. Contact the controller about data from a previous installation.

Purchasing the full version

Google Play processes payments through Unity IAP. The game receives the information needed to verify the product and transaction and save the full-version entitlement. We do not receive your card number or Google Account login details. The store processes payment information under its own privacy policy.

Unity IAP may also send transaction and receipt data, identifiers, device information and diagnostics to its provider to operate and measure purchases. This is separate from the optional gameplay analytics described above. See Unity IAP privacy information for details. Purchases in Dead Fuse use Google Play.

The in-game analytics deletion button applies only to Unity Analytics. Requests relating to Unity IAP purchase-service data require separate handling. You can send them to the game's controller; under Unity's procedure, we forward verified requests concerning data processed on our behalf. When Unity processes data for its own purposes, requests must be sent directly to Unity. See Unity IAP access and deletion information.

Service providers and retention

The game uses Unity services, including LevelPlay, Unity Ads and Unity Analytics, and Google services, including AdMob, UMP and Google Play. Data may also be processed outside the European Economic Area, under the rules and transfer mechanisms used by these providers.

Local data remains on your device until it is deleted or replaced. Unity states that raw Analytics events are retained for 13 months. Analytics metrics and user profiles may be retained until the player or developer requests deletion. You can request earlier deletion in the game's privacy settings. See the Unity Analytics retention documentation.

Unity states that IAP service logs are retained for 90 days and order records until deletion is requested. Separate accounting and security obligations may affect what can be deleted. These periods do not describe Google Play payment-record retention.

Advertising and store providers retain data according to its purpose and their policies. Transaction data may also be subject to accounting and fraud-prevention requirements.

Your choices and rights

Optional analytics and advertising processing that requires consent depend on your choices. Data needed for a purchase is used to fulfil the order and manage the entitlement. You can contact the controller to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or to object to processing, as provided by applicable law. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. You can also complain to the relevant supervisory authority.

Visits to this website

This game's information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics tools or advertising to it. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website traffic is separate from data collected by the game.

Audience and changes

The game is not directed at children under 13. Significant changes to data processing will be reflected in this policy and, where required, in a new consent choice in the app.
